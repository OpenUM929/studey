## Arbiter's own new finding — C1 (P2's numbers are not safe to install)

P2 would write "배점 2.1~3.6점, 서답형 2~10점". The measured distinct values include
**2.0** (24기말, 25중간, 25기말) and **4.0** (24기말, 25기말), and the extraction does not
separate 선택형 from 서답형 point marks. So "2.1" as the lower bound and "3.6" as the upper
bound are both unproven — one is likely too high and the other likely too low. Installing
them would replace a wrong number with another wrong number, which is the same failure
mode ruling 08/Q1 rejects.
