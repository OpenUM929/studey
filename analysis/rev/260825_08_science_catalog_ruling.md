## Arbiter's own new finding — B1 (Q1 cannot be answered with option (a))

Per-round selected-response point bands, measured from the answer keys and scans:

| 24중간 | 24기말 | 25중간 | 25기말 | 26중간 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.2~2.8 | 3.0~3.6 | 2.3~2.7 | 3.0~3.9 | **3.4~4.0** (p02/p04: 3.4·3.6·3.8·4.0) |

The catalog's current claim ("2024 = 2.2~2.8, 2025~2026 = 3.0~4.0") is wrong for 24기말.
But the proposed replacement (a) — "중간 2.2~2.8 · 기말 3.0~3.6, 회차 구분이 연도 구분보다
강함" — is **also wrong**, because 26중간 sits at 3.4~4.0. The midterm/final pattern holds
for 2024 and 2025 and then **breaks in 2026** (the band was raised). Adopting (a) would
replace one false generalisation with another and would erase the single most
forecast-relevant fact in the row: the 2026 band shift.
